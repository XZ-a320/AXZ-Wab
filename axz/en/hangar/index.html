Air Xiao Ze (AXZ) · Hangar

The fleet, rolled into a hangar you can walk around. Every aircraft comes in two models, switched below the picture. The 3.0 model is a sourced model with a real licence and a real author, downloaded from the asset library, and it is the one simulator 3.0 flies. The 2.0 model is computed in code from published dimensions when the page opens, with no model file and no textures. The four Air Xiao Ze aircraft come first; the hangar's guests follow.

Model

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan; the arrow keys pan too.

Moving parts: the gear cycles, the flaps run out and back, the control surfaces swing and the fans turn, one loop every 24 seconds. Only 3.0 models with animation files have this.

Where the 3.0 models come from

1.0 and 2.0 built every airframe from published dimensions and downloaded nothing. 3.0 downloads real models instead, and says where each one came from. The best of them are FlightGear's, under the GPL: the converted files and the converter are published in the assets repository, so anyone may take them onward on the same terms. The Sketchfab models are under CC BY, and each one's credit names its author and what was changed.

Where the 2.0 models come from

There are no model files. Each aircraft is computed with Three.js from published dimensions when the page opens: the fuselage is a dozen superellipse cross-sections joined by a spline into one surface, the wings, tail and blades are airfoils lofted along the span, and the cabin windows are laid onto the computed skin at the real 508 mm pitch. For the four Air Xiao Ze aircraft the length, span, height, wing area and track come from the same data as the fleet page and the simulator; every other type uses the manufacturer's figures. Three.js loads from a CDN, and the download starts only once the hangar scrolls into view.
