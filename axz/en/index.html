Fleet in Service

All Aircraft · Fleet overview (by entry into service)

Passenger aircraft

Registration B-737X

Boeing 737-800

B-737X was Air Xiao Ze's first aircraft, and the type the company launched with. Through the first months of 2026 it flew every AXZ001/AXZ002 rotation on KSFO↔KSNS by itself. It saw the airline start from nothing, and it is still a veteran mainstay of the fleet.

Notable Events

  1. Operated AXZ001, the first flight in Air Xiao Ze's history.
  2. The fleet's only aircraft in the opening period, flying several round trips a day.
  3. Completed abnormal-procedures training: crosswinds, engine-out handling, rejected takeoffs.
😂 Incidents

Lands steadily, if a little on the "hard" side. The ground crew calls it the Runway Shaker.

Once flew the wrong route and had to loop back around. The FO quietly screenshotted it and turned it into a meme.

Holds the highest standing in the fleet. Everyone has to call it the old-timer.

Registration B-321X

Airbus A321

B-321X is the second mainstay passenger type Air Xiao Ze brought in, an Airbus A321. Its job is to take pressure off B-737X and add frequencies, and it flies the core AXZ001/AXZ002 rotations. It carries a good share of the passenger flying.

Notable Events

  1. Frequencies on the route went up sharply once it entered service.
  2. Has flown a number of extra sections at peak times.
  3. Went along while crews ran abnormal-procedures drills, including a simulated engine failure.
😂 Incidents

Barely arrived before it put in a hard landing. People joked it had come to take someone's job.

On one training flight the crew forgot the flaps and flew the approach anyway. The whole cabin went awkwardly quiet for a full minute.

It calls itself the elegant people-mover. In practice the landings are rough now and then.

Registration B-1717

Boeing 737-800 (MC collab)

B-1717 is Air Xiao Ze's collab aircraft with Minecraft, and it swore it would never lose its paint!

Notable Events

  1. Entered service as the first Air Xiao Ze collab aircraft.
  2. Completed its first KSFO↔KSNS passenger flight on March 20, 2026.
  3. It is also the best-maintained aircraft in the Air Xiao Ze fleet.
😂 Incidents

While the livery was being sprayed, a waterproofing problem made it lose its paint.

Had a hard landing on day one of its inaugural service.

On June 11, 2026 the captain pushed straight through the turbulence. Passengers said it felt like they'd been "painted" onto the cabin wall.

Cargo aircraft

Registration B-0001F

Boeing 737-800BCF

B-0001F is Air Xiao Ze's first dedicated freighter, a 737-800BCF. It flies the virtual cargo services, carrying simulated supplies and small parcels. With it the airline started running passenger and cargo operations side by side.

Notable Events

  1. Completed Air Xiao Ze's first commercial cargo flight.
  2. Passed demanding tests: takeoffs and landings at heavy weights, and simulated off-center loading.
  3. Has flown night cargo services steadily over a long period.
😂 Incidents

A loading error sent the nose up far too sharply. People joked that "the cargo was making a break for it."

Taxis so slowly that the tower keeps telling it to expedite.

Does the fleet's grunt work. Hauls the boxes, never says a word.

Air Xiao Ze · The Full Record

Airline History

Air Xiao Ze (AXZ) was founded in 2026, with its main base at San Francisco International Airport (KSFO). In its early period the company ran one premium return route, KSFO ↔ KSNS, with the Boeing 737-800 as its only type. The A321 passenger type came in later, then the 737-800BCF freighter. That gave the airline a lean, efficient fleet carrying both passengers and cargo. It is a niche airline that concentrates on flying. Some of the main fleet later moved to the Chinese market, and the ZSPD ↔ ZSNJ return route opened.

Notable Events

  1. Formally founded in 2026. B-737X operated AXZ001, the inaugural flight.
  2. Brought in A321 B-321X to expand passenger capacity and add frequencies.
  3. Put the newly acquired B-1717 into a long-running collab with Minecraft to raise the airline's profile.
  4. Freighter B-0001F entered service and opened Air Xiao Ze's cargo business.
  5. A full year of safe simulated operations, with no major accidents.
😂 Incidents We'd Rather Not Discuss

On the day of the inaugural flight the captain taxied over the speed limit and took one warning after another from virtual ATC. An instant classic.

B-737X has put in a number of gentle hard landings and was duly awarded the title "the Runway Masseur."

The A321 had barely entered service before it delivered the forgot-the-flaps classic. The whole crew went quiet.

Freighter B-0001F pitched up much too hard because the loading was off, and the joke went round that "the parcels were eloping."
